# Reconcile Ledgers and Build Excel

1. Inspect both workbooks before matching. Record sheets, headers, date coverage, amount fields, balances, formulas, and original source rows.
2. Establish the overlapping comparison period. Exclude later transactions as non-comparable items and disclose them separately.
3. Normalise gross amount, actual amount, discount, refund, fee, tax, weight adjustment, occurrence, and payment fields.
4. Match by document reference, exact amount, exact date, then business description.
5. Separate opening carryover, cross-period corrections, and current-period new variances.
6. For every non-zero item, show both parties' amounts, the formula-driven difference, the exact calculation, the cause, and the required follow-up.
7. Preserve full source precision and round only the final currency presentation.
8. Reconcile detailed exceptions to total occurrences and same-cut-off ending balances.
9. Produce management detail, disaggregated audit detail, and a verification sheet. Use formulas for differences, subtotals, cumulative totals, and consistency checks.
10. Preserve the source workbooks unchanged and never publish confidential source data.
